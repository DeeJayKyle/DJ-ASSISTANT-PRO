DJ Assistant 2.1.1

**Auto-import: new music goes straight into your library.**

- **Turn it on in Settings → Auto-import.** Music you buy or download (Beatport, Bandcamp, Juno, promo and record pools) is picked up from your downloads folder as soon as it has finished downloading.
- **Copied, never moved.** Each new track is copied into a "DJ Assistant Inbox" folder in your Music folder (a folder per day) and checked byte for byte. Nothing in your downloads folder is moved, changed or deleted.
- **ZIPs unpacked for you.** Bandcamp and Beatport bundles are unpacked, audio only; covers and other files are left out.
- **Ready to prep.** New tracks are scanned, analysed (BPM, key, beatgrid), tidied by auto-prep and added to an **Inbox** crate. Nothing is imported twice, and auto-import pauses in gig mode.
- Choose both folders, press **Check now**, and see recent imports in the same panel.

Everything from 2.1.0 is included: whole-file beatgrids, BPM range, prepared stems, Serato grid import and the new DJ Decks.

**Please note:** the installer is signed for automatic updates, but not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show an "unknown publisher" warning.
